One synthetic attempt with independent human and agent production branches. Human production takes 2 hours, unattended agent execution takes 8 hours, and human verification takes 1.5 hours after both finish. The lower panel integrates human allocation: h = 2 + 1.5 = 3.5 person-hours. The elapsed critical path is t = max(2, 8) + 1.5 = 9.5 hours. Agent execution adds no human labour during the unattended interval. This drawing excludes the initial queue, retries and follow-up repair; it is a constructed schedule, not an observed workflow.
