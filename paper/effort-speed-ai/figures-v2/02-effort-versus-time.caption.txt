Four synthetic workflows, with expected human labour to first acceptance and expected elapsed time to first acceptance divided by their respective human-baseline means (7.3684 person-hours and 9.3684 hours). Sequential independent attempts continue to the first pass; each scenario has the same one-off 2-hour initial wait. Fast and slow agents require equal expected human labour, but the slow agent increases elapsed time above baseline. Heavy review increases both totals. Values are deterministic model expectations, not estimates or confidence intervals; follow-up repair labour is excluded. Scenario parameters and exact totals are in the accompanying table.
