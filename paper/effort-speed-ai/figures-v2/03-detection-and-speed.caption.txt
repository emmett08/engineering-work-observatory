Detection sensitivity s varies from 0 to 1 in a synthetic model with independent attempts, defect probability d = 0.4, no false-positive rejection, ten reference units per task, and two elapsed hours per attempt. Sequential retries stop at the first pass; queueing and subsequent repair are excluded. Recorded accepted throughput is 5 − 2s reference units per hour, correct-output throughput is 3 units per hour, and correctness conditional on acceptance is 0.6/(1 − 0.4s). Weakening detection increases recorded throughput and reduces accepted correctness, while correct-output throughput is unchanged under these particular assumptions. Curves are derived functions, not observed effects; correctness is distinct from the article’s retained-functionality fraction.
