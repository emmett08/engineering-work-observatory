One person spends one hour on task A and one hour on task B over two hours. Panel (a) plots hours spent on A in the first hour on the horizontal axis and hours spent on B in that hour on the vertical axis. Each axis runs from zero to one. The shaded square shows the pairs allowed by the separate task bounds. The diagonal labelled “Feasible pairs” joins the two ways to spend the entire first hour on one task. Above the line the tasks need more than an hour in the first hour. Below it too much work remains for the second hour. The midpoint P marks half an hour on each task.

Panel (b) plots the combined first-hour labour in person-hours. Adding the separate bounds gives a horizontal bar from zero to two. The diamond labelled “One person’s capacity” fixes the sum at one. Panel (c) plots elapsed hours from zero to two. Four consecutive half-hour blocks alternate A, B, A, B. The divider at one hour shows how this schedule realises P.
