One person spends one hour on task A and one hour on task B over a two-hour shift. In panel (a), the horizontal axis gives first-hour labour on A and the vertical axis gives first-hour labour on B, each from zero to one person-hour. The shaded square shows the pairs allowed by the separate bounds. The diagonal labelled “Feasible pairs” runs between (0,1) and (1,0). Above it the work exceeds one person's first-hour capacity. Below it more than an hour of work remains for the second hour. The midpoint P marks half an hour on each task.

In panel (b), a horizontal bar runs from zero to two person-hours, the range obtained by adding the separate bounds. The diamond labelled “One person’s capacity” marks the feasible sum of one person-hour. In panel (c), four consecutive half-hour blocks cover the two-hour shift in the order A, B, A, B. Before the one-hour divider each task occupies half an hour, giving the two coordinates of P.
