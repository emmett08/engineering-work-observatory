Constructed example of one person spending one person-hour on each of two tasks during two hours. The left plot has first-hour task A labour on the horizontal axis and first-hour task B labour on the vertical axis, both from zero to one person-hour. A light square contains all pairs permitted by the separate bounds. A dark descending diagonal from zero, one to one, zero contains the jointly feasible pairs. Pairs above it exceed first-hour capacity; pairs below it require excess work in the second hour. The right plot shows that adding separate bounds permits a combined first-hour total from zero to two person-hours, while joint constraints permit only one. These marks describe possible totals, not probabilities or complete work schedules.
