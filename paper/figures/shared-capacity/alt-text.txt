Constructed example of one person spending one person-hour on each of two tasks during two hours. Panel (a) has first-hour task A labour on the horizontal axis and first-hour task B labour on the vertical axis, both from zero to one person-hour. A light square contains all pairs permitted by the separate bounds. A dark descending diagonal from zero, one to one, zero contains the jointly feasible pairs. Pairs above it exceed first-hour capacity; pairs below it require excess work in the second hour. A diamond marks P, where both first-hour task totals equal half a person-hour. Panel (b) shows that adding separate bounds permits a combined first-hour total from zero to two person-hours, while joint constraints permit only one. Panel (c) supplies one possible full-attention schedule for P: Task A from 0 to 0.5 hours, B from 0.5 to 1, A from 1 to 1.5, and B from 1.5 to 2. A dashed divider marks the first-hour boundary. Every interval contains one activity at a time. The timeline is a constructed witness allowing arbitrary interruption, not a unique or observed history. No panel encodes probability or a causal effect.
