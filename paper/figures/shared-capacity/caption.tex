\textbf{First-hour labour when two tasks fill one person's shift.}
The engineer spends one hour on each task over a two-hour shift. In (a), the shaded square shows the first-hour totals $(x,y)$ that each task's bounds allow separately. The two tasks fill both hours, so only the diagonal $x+y=1$ gives feasible pairs.
In (b), the bar spans the sums allowed by the separate bounds, from zero to two person-hours. Every pair on the diagonal sums to the one person-hour marked by the diamond.
In (c), alternating tasks every half-hour gives one schedule for the midpoint $P=(0.5,0.5)$.
