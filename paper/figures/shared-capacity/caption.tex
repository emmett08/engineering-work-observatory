\textbf{First-hour labour when two tasks fill one person's shift.}
Over two hours the engineer spends one hour on each task. In (a), the square contains all pairs of first-hour totals $(x,y)$ allowed by the two individual bounds. The two task totals use the entire shift, so the feasible pairs lie on the diagonal $x+y=1$.
In (b), adding the individual bounds gives $[0,2]$ person-hours. The diagonal projects to a single total of one person-hour.
The four half-hour blocks in (c) give one schedule for the midpoint $P=(0.5,0.5)$. Other switching times can give the same totals.
