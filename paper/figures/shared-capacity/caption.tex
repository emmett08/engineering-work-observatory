\textbf{Shared capacity determines the combined first-hour labour.}
Constructed example: one person spends one person-hour on each of two tasks during two hours.
In (a), $x$ and $y$ are the tasks' first-hour labour totals.
The shaded square satisfies the separate bounds; only the diagonal satisfies the person's capacity in both hours.
In (b), adding the separate bounds permits between zero and two person-hours, whereas the joint constraints require exactly one.
The plotted points specify interval totals, not complete work schedules.
