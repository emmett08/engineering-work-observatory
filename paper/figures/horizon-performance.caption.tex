Observed elapsed times for repeated cohort-horizon queries on generated arrays of 100,000 accepted-output records and 400,000 labour increments. Markers are medians. Whiskers show the minimum and maximum across 15 paired runs (three seeds, five fresh-process repetitions per seed). They are not confidence intervals. Indexed totals include construction, queries and disposal. All one-query pairs favoured scanning and all 100-query pairs favoured indexing. Ten-query pairs varied in direction. Only four query counts were timed, so the connecting lines do not locate break-even. This result is confined to the generated workload and execution environment and does not estimate human labour or an effect of an AI intervention.
