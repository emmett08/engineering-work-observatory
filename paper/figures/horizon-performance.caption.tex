Measured execution times for calculating totals of weighted scores and labour at repeated acceptance deadlines. One generated array contains 100,000 acceptance ages paired with scores. The other contains 400,000 labour interval end ages paired with labour weights. Markers are medians. Whiskers show the minimum and maximum across 15 paired runs (three seeds, five fresh-process repetitions per seed). They are not confidence intervals. Indexed totals include construction, queries and disposal. All one-query pairs favoured scanning and all 100-query pairs favoured indexing. At ten queries, scanning was faster in 4 pairs and indexing was faster in 11. Only four query counts were timed, so the connecting lines leave the exact query count at which indexing becomes faster unresolved. The plotted durations describe computation on generated integers. Estimating human labour or an effect of an AI intervention requires separate measurements.
