Observed elapsed times for calculating output and labour totals at repeated acceptance deadlines, using generated arrays of 100,000 output records and 400,000 labour contributions. Markers are medians. Whiskers show the minimum and maximum across 15 paired runs (three seeds, five fresh-process repetitions per seed). They are not confidence intervals. Indexed totals include construction, queries and disposal. All one-query pairs favoured scanning and all 100-query pairs favoured indexing. At ten queries, scanning was faster in 4 pairs and indexing was faster in 11. Only four query counts were timed, so the connecting lines leave the exact query count at which indexing becomes faster unresolved. This result is confined to the generated workload and execution environment and does not estimate human labour or an effect of an AI intervention.
