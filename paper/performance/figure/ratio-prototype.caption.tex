Alternative composition of the same retained benchmark pairs. Each marker is
the median of within-pair scan time divided by indexed total time; whiskers are
the observed minimum and maximum across 15 paired runs (three generator seeds,
five fresh-process repetitions per seed). Indexed time includes construction,
queries and disposal. Ratios above one favour indexing. The ten-query range
crosses one. The generated workload contains 100,000 accepted-output events and
400,000 labour increments. Absolute elapsed times are not displayed in this
composition; neither an exact break-even count nor a human-labour effect is
estimated.
