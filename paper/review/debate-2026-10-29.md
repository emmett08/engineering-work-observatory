## Findings not promoted into corrections

- The main analytical propositions survived the reviewed counterexamples under
  their stated hypotheses. Existing qualifications already address retry-history
  quality, abandonment, unequal cluster sizes, reactive quality audits and the
  difference between finite-cohort and eventual-cycle ratios.
- A preliminary Cunningham-name criticism was withdrawn: the bibliography already
  matches the source. Van Der Post's supplied metadata remains unverified and is
  still used only as further reading.
- The original finite-history Lean declaration covers every positive finite
  length; a concern about that quantifier was withdrawn after source inspection.
- Reference-weighted component retention is useful if a rubric promises regrouping
  invariance. The current paper requires a declared rubric and makes no such
  universal promise, so that optional extension was not imposed.
- Predictable-stopping and queue examples are useful further sensitivity analyses,
  but were not needed to repair the current statements or observation contract.
- Moving the authorised performance extension to an appendix was an editorial
  option. Its current placement and explicit scope were retained.
