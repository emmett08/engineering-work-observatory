## Exact strengthening: full attention suffices

Let the finite stream set for one person have S=|I||R| elements. Keep the baseline finite partition b_0<...<b_K, all observations, all target weights constant on cells, and every feasible cell vector x in P. Then there exists a Borel step-function history with a_ijr(t) in {0,1} and sum_(i,r) a_ijr(t)<=1 at every time (up to harmless domain-endpoint conventions) that reproduces **every** x_ijrk simultaneously. Consequently the image of the full-attention histories under all cell-integral observations and any finite collection of cell-constant linear targets equals the image of P. This strengthens, rather than invalidates, the baseline LP result.

Proof: fix an order s=1,...,S for person j's streams. In cell k put t_0=b_(k-1) and t_s=b_(k-1)+sum_(u<=s) x_ujk. Capacity ensures t_S<=b_k. Set stream s to 1 on [t_(s-1),t_s), all other streams to 0 there, and idle on [t_S,b_k). Repeat independently for every person and cell. Zero-length blocks are omitted. Each integral is precisely its block length x_sjk. Each person's cell has at most S+1 nonempty constant pieces, so the entire domain has at most K(S+1) pieces and at most K(S+1)-1 internal switching times for that person. The construction does not need integrality of the person-hour totals.

This gives a concrete counterexample to a literal reading of the sentence “This exact correspondence depends on allowing measurable fractional allocations.” For one person, one unit cell, and task totals 1/2,1/2, half-time simultaneous allocation is unnecessary: work exclusively on A during [0,1/2) and exclusively on B during [1/2,1). The general construction proves the same for every feasible cell vector.

Suggested replacement: “The same cell totals can also be realised by assigning each person wholly to one stream at a time, with finitely many switches. The correspondence permits work to start, stop and change streams at arbitrary times; minimum session lengths, restrictions on interruption, smoothness requirements or switching costs require separate analysis.” Do not suggest the construction preserves a bound on the number of sessions, task precedence, uninterrupted tasks, time-varying coefficients inside cells, or delays needed to resume work.

A sharp boundary example for interruption: use one person and three unit cells. Require A totals (1/2,0,1/2) and B totals (0,1,0) through exact cell observations. The LP is feasible, with full-attention blocks as constructed, but A cannot occupy a single contiguous session because B occupies the entire middle cell. Thus replacing fractional attention by full attention preserves the targets; adding nonpreemption can destroy feasibility.

Published comparison: Dvoretzky, Wald and Wolfowitz (1951), “Elimination of Randomization in Certain Statistical Decision Procedures and Zero-Sum Two-Person Games”, Annals of Mathematical Statistics 22(1), 1–21, doi:10.1214/aoms/1177729689. Crossref confirms this metadata. Khan, Rath and Sun (2006), “The Dvoretzky-Wald-Wolfowitz theorem and purification in atomless finite-action games”, International Journal of Game Theory 34, 91–104, doi:10.1007/s00182-005-0004-3, has an accessible publisher abstract stating that a mixed strategy into a finite action space has a pure strategy with the same integrals for finitely many atomless measures. This is a related general purification result. I inspected that abstract and bibliographic metadata, not a full theorem proof, so I do not certify the most general hypotheses from that source. The elementary per-cell construction here proves the required restricted statement directly and avoids importing game-theoretic machinery. Suggested use: optional attribution, no novelty claim and no dependence on the general theorem.

## Stronger simplifications that fail

1. **Replace the general joint LP by a scalar difference-constraints graph.** With one person, one cell of duration 2, and three streams x1,x2,x3, observe x1+x2=x1+x3=x2+x3=1. The unique solution is (1/2,1/2,1/2), within capacity since the sum is 3/2. The observation matrix [[1,1,0],[1,0,1],[0,1,1]] has determinant -2. Thus the permitted grouped observations are not generally totally unimodular, even with integral input durations and labour totals. This refutes a direct claim that the original cell variables always form an integral network-flow/difference-constraints polytope. It does not prove that every possible auxiliary-variable graph representation is impossible. Full-attention histories still realise this example: stream 1 for half an hour, stream 2 for half an hour, stream 3 for half an hour, then half an hour idle. Fractional cell totals and fractional instantaneous allocation are different restrictions.

2. **Optimise each stream independently and add.** The baseline already contains an exact counterexample: A=B=1 over [0,2] for one person implies total first-hour labour exactly 1, while separate scalar ranges give [0,2]. Shared capacity is the missing coupling. A scalar graph cannot be deployed independently per stream unless constraints cease to link those streams; simply retaining their individual capacity bounds is insufficient.

3. **Discard cells outside the target.** With one person, one stream, observed total 1 over [0,2], target [0,1], the true target range is [0,1]. Reassigning the whole observation to the target yields the false point value 1. This remains false with precise measurements. The outside cells encode where the observed work may have occurred.

4. **Treat robustness as satisfying every possible exact observation value.** If a measured scalar is justified only to lie in [0,1], partial identification permits x in [0,1]: the true observation value is unknown but one value is realised. Requiring x=v for every v in [0,1] gives the empty set. For uncertain right-hand sides, the union over possible truths and the intersection of feasible histories solve different problems. The existing lower/upper observation bands already implement the intended union in this simple case. Correlation between observation errors needs a joint uncertainty set; independent bands can be an outer approximation.

5. **Claim bounded sensitivity without a denominator margin.** For fixed output Q=1 and an exact labour observation H=epsilon>0, productivity is 1/epsilon. Even arbitrarily small absolute labour changes can have arbitrarily large ratio effects near zero. Positive denominators for each individual instance do not give a uniform robustness claim. A fixed positive lower bound is needed for such a claim. Infeasibility is also sensitive at capacity: exact stream totals 1/2 and 1/2 in one unit cell are feasible, whereas increasing either by any positive amount makes them inconsistent.

## A valid optional simplification: merge equal columns

For fixed person j and cell k, associate to each stream its column of observation coefficients and its weights for every target to be retained. Group streams only if these coefficient vectors are identical. Replace each group C by y_C=sum_(s in C) x_s. Give y_C its common observation and target coefficients, with y_C>=0 and sum_C y_C<=d_k. This is an exact quotient of the cell LP for the declared targets.

Proof: summing any original solution produces a reduced solution with the same observations and targets. Conversely, put all y_C on one representative stream from each class and set other members to zero. Capacity and every retained coefficient sum are unchanged; the baseline reconstruction, or the full-attention construction above, then gives a history. The proof requires the members to share the same person and cell; pooling across persons without additional equivalence analysis can erase constraints. New target weights may split previously merged classes. No variables are saved in the worst case. This is useful preprocessing when roles or tasks are observationally indistinguishable, but it need not replace the main exposition with more notation.

## Sampling and ratio consequences

The variance criterion must retain the actual design. A smaller unweighted residual sum of squares alone need not improve design precision. Exact counterexample: M=2, select exactly one unit with probabilities (9/10,1/10), y=(1,1), fixed f=(1,0), H=2. HT estimates are (10/9,10) and variance 64/9; difference estimates are (1,11) and variance 9. Residual sum of squares falls from 2 to 1 while design variance increases. Both estimates have expectation 2. This refutes an overly broad “better predictions improve precision” shortcut, while agreeing with the baseline's covariance formula and SRS-specific residual-variance criterion.

Joint output/labour restrictions cannot be replaced by marginal interval arithmetic if exactness matters. If H ranges over [1,2] and Q=H is required, both marginals are [1,2] but the true productivity range is {1}; the independent-envelope calculation gives [1/2,2]. The baseline correctly calls this only an enclosing interval absent joint feasibility.

If H_min=0, do not automatically assert an unbounded ratio: Q=H on H in [0,1], restricted to positive H, still gives ratio 1. If Q=1 independently and H in [0,1], positive-denominator ratios are [1,infinity), without a finite attained maximum. If H_max=0 no productivity ratio is defined. A joint linear-fractional formulation is useful only after jointly uncertain Q and H have been specified; it buys nothing for fixed Q and a known strictly positive labour range beyond the existing reversed endpoints.
