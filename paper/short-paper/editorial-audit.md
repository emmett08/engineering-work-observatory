# Editorial audit

The review covers the main text, headings, captions and table prose. It excludes equations, LaTeX commands, citation keys, URLs and the bibliography. Counts are case-insensitive word forms after removing function words. Inflections are counted separately. The table records decisions after revision.

## Negative Syndeton

None found in the final pass. The introduction now specifies which work a labour measurement must include. An earlier draft used “Neither quantity measures” to dismiss two related measures in one sentence.

## Contrastive Framing

The work-hours and commit example compares 30 expected active minutes with 15 under the same commit law. Keep the comparison because it establishes the identifiability result. The results table compares equal range widths with different coverage. Its comparison is the evidence for retaining uncertain review time.

## Tetracolon

None found in the final pass. The figure and table carry their defined sets of cases directly.

## Grammatical Parallelism

The bounds section uses “The first period has to take” and “It cannot take more” to explain the lower and upper limits. Each sentence ties a different endpoint to available time. The trial section uses one upper limit and one lower limit because the two outcomes have different directions of benefit.

## Syndeton

Necessary coordination remains where the reasoning needs it. “The feasible set is closed and bounded” states the two properties used to justify existence of extrema. The benefit decision requires both a labour saving and an acceptable retained fraction, so the conjunction stays explicit. The audit paragraph separates what an auditor times from what a reviewer tests.

## Over-elaboration

- The opening uses the timing of the review to explain why the first-half total becomes exactly one hour. The rewrite removes “This illustrates the distinction that drives the paper”.
- The results section now derives its count from “24 engineer schedules with four possible review slots”. This replaces three sentences that introduced the counts, weights and reference totals separately.
- The estimation section uses the monotonicity of the weighted total to explain how lower and upper inputs propagate. This replaces repeated “substituting ... gives” clauses.
- The trial section moves directly from the false claim to independent workplace measurement. The revision drops a sentence that restated that the thresholds had been crossed.
